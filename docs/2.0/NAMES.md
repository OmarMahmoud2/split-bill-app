All five .com/.app exact domains are taken (these are common words; expected). Plan on a fallback domain or buying the exact one.

### 1. Equally (recommended)
- Meaning: split things equally; also "equal footing" between friends. Instantly guessable, warm, non-technical.
- Pronunciation: EE-kwuh-lee (3 syl, /ˈiːkwəli/). Slightly harder for Arabic speakers (q/kw) but common word; Arabic wordmark can transliterate.
- Store: title "Equally: Split Bills & Scan" (27). Subtitle (30): "Receipts, voice, settle up" (26) or "Scan, say who had what, settle" (30).
- Risks: 7 letters/3 syllables (soft miss on brief); 31 chars with the standard template; descriptive adjective = weaker trademark, and "equally" is wrong for non-equal splits (item-level splitting is the product's strength); Equals Money (UK fintech, different word) is a mild confusion; USPTO/EUIPO unverified.
- Icon: teal rounded square with two stacked equal horizontal bars (an equals sign) the lower bar slightly offset like a ledger rule.
- Splash: canvas #F7F7F5, a single teal equals mark draws its two lines in sequence, then the wordmark in ink fades up.

### 2. Fairly
- Meaning: "fairly" = justly/equitably; fair split. Short, friendly, sounds like "fair-ly".
- Pronunciation: FAIR-lee (2 syl). Easy globally; r/l is the only hurdle.
- Store: "Fairly: Split Bills & Receipts" (30, exactly at limit). Subtitle: "Scan, tell who had what, settle" (30).
- Risks: also means "somewhat/moderately" in English (slightly weak brand connotation: "fairly good"); "Fairly Staffing" (Business) in US store; fairlyapp.com/fairlypay.com registered 2025 (someone may be building a product); FairShare cluster of split apps nearby; Fairly.com taken. TM unverified.
- Icon: teal circle split by a thin vertical hairline into two exactly equal halves (balance, no scale clipart).
- Splash: ink hairline draws down the canvas, a teal circle opens around it, wordmark appears.

### 3. Kitty
- Meaning: shared pot of money (UK/AU/US idiom, "chip into the kitty"); very memorable, 2 syl.
- Pronunciation: KIT-ee. Trivially easy.
- Store: "Kitty: Split Bills & Receipts" (29). Subtitle: "Scan, share, settle up" or "Pay-back made calm" (<=30).
- Risks: cat connotation fights "Quiet ledger/premium"; idiom unknown to many non-native users (so not self-explanatory in EU/Gulf); crowded brand (Hello Kitty, Kitty terminal, crypto/other apps - exact Play search unverified); kitty.app registered. TM unverified, likely contested.
- Icon: teal circle with a flat horizontal slot (a coin-pot) in ink; no cat.
- Splash: coins as small teal dots drop into a hairline circle, ring closes, wordmark appears.

### 4. Evens
- Meaning: "we're even/evens" (settled up between friends); also "evens" = odds 1:1.
- Pronunciation: EE-venz (2 syl).
- Store: "Evens: Split Bills & Receipts" (29). Subtitle: "Scan, say who had what, settle" (30).
- Risks: plural reads odd; sits in a crowded "Even-" cluster (Even Financial / even.com, Evenly, EvenUp, EvenX, EvenSplit, SplitEven): high confusion risk; evens.com/.app taken (evenspay.com free). TM likely contested.
- Icon: teal rounded square with a single ink equals-sign-like pair of unequal-then-equal bars (balance resolved).
- Splash: two bars slide to level, then wordmark.

### 5. Evenhand
- Meaning: from "even-handed" = fair, impartial. Clear compound.
- Pronunciation: EE-ven-hand (3 syl, 8 letters).
- Store: "Evenhand: Split Bills & Scan" (28). Subtitle: "Fair splits, calm balances" (26).
- Risks: three syllables, a little formal, "hand" is hard for some (h dropped by some speakers); evenhand.com/.app registered; fewer cultures know "even-handed". TM unverified.
- Icon: two matched teal half-circles forming an open hand-less "level" lens, thin ink baseline.
- Splash: baseline draws, the two halves rise to meet, wordmark appears.

## Recommendation
Equally. It is the only top candidate that is a plain adjective everyone understands, has no exact-name app in US/GB/DE/SA, and pairs naturally with "amounts as the hero" (equals sign mark). Cost: 7 letters / 3 syllables and a 31-char template (use "Equally: Split Bills & Scan"). If a 2-syllable name that fits the template exactly matters more, pick Fairly. Neither exact .com/.app is free; secure getequally.com / equallypay.com now (free as of today).
