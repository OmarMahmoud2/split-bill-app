Questions people ask at the table

Do my friends need the app?

No. Each friend gets a link that opens in any browser with their share and your payment details.

How accurate is the scan?

It reads clear receipts line by line, including tax and service. You see every line before the bill is made, and can fix anything in a tap.

Does Evens move money?

No. Friends pay you directly with PayPal, Venmo, InstaPay or whatever you use. Evens keeps track of who has paid.

Which languages and currencies?

22 languages, including Arabic and Urdu written right to left, and any currency, with several on one trip in Pro.

What happens to my receipt photos?

They are used to read the receipt and are not used to train AI models. You can delete your account and data from the app at any time.
